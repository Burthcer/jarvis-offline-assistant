**This does not solve phone interference.** Synthetic other voices are not a real-world false-accept estimate. Overlapping owner/other speech at equal loudness rejected the owner sample at the fixed threshold; identifying a voice does not remove the other person's words. A recording of the owner is not distinguishable as secure authentication, and a short wake-only sound is insufficient. Keep the experiment disabled until real false-accept, missed-command and speed tests justify a concrete design.

Model/source: [SpeechBrain voice-check model](https://huggingface.co/speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb). Local download pins the upstream revision, verifies size/SHA-256 and loads only tensor weights. The CPU export has its own checksum and decision-parity check.
