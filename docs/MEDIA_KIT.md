## One-line pitches

- *An offline voice assistant that never sends a word off your laptop.*
- *Say "Jarvis, click Shorts." It does, and it tells you the truth about whether it worked.*

## Promo video storyboard (about 45 seconds)

Record with the real app on a clean demo profile, no personal facts on screen, and mute any music before filming.

| Time | Shot | Voice-over or on-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:04 | Black screen, the reactor fades in and breathes | "This laptop is listening." |
| 0:04 to 0:10 | "Hey Jarvis. Open YouTube." Window opens; reactor pulses | "No cloud. No account." |
| 0:10 to 0:17 | "Set a timer for ten minutes." Timer appears on Home | "It does the everyday things instantly." |
| 0:17 to 0:26 | On a YouTube page: "Number the screen", then "click 12" | "And now it can see your screen." |
| 0:26 to 0:33 | "Reply to Sam that I'm running late." Draft appears, Jarvis reads it back, "Send" | "It drafts. You decide." |
| 0:33 to 0:39 | Knowledge page scrolling, then "Forget it all" button | "It learns you. You stay in control." |
| 0:39 to 0:45 | Timeline graphic, then the banner | "Jarvis V4. Offline. Yours." |

Keep the screen-click shots on public, signed-out pages. Add the live recording to `docs/media/` later (large video files are better hosted elsewhere and linked).
